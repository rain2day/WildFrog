# WildFrog App Preview Design

## Style Prompt

Warm Topographic Passport: a premium Hong Kong hiking passport trailer built from real in-app screens. The video should feel like a tactile field logbook meeting an App Store product preview: warm paper, forest ink, trail-orange action accents, map contour lines, stamp/certificate moments, and crisp editorial pacing. The UI remains the hero; motion adds depth, hierarchy, and polish without becoming a generic pitch deck.

## Colors

- Paper: `#F6F0E2` for the primary canvas and warm surfaces.
- Passport: `#EFE3C8` for cards, certificates, and stamp panels.
- Forest: `#153E2F` for primary type, rules, and icon accents.
- Moss: `#647B48` for secondary completion accents.
- Trail Orange: `#D45B2B` for CTAs, transitions, and moment highlights.
- Line: `#D7CBB3` for subtle borders and map-style separators.
- Sky: `#A8C4CF` for cool counterpoint behind mountain imagery.

## Typography

- Display voice: `Fraunces`, `Songti TC`, serif. Use for large scene titles and poetic short phrases.
- Product/data voice: `Avenir Next`, `PingFang TC`, sans-serif. Use for captions, stats, labels, and UI-adjacent text.
- Data microcopy: `JetBrains Mono`, monospace. Use sparingly for coordinates, peak counts, and App Store-preview style metadata.

## Motion Rules

- Use editorial push/blur/light-leak transitions, not jump cuts.
- Every scene gets staggered entrances with varied easing and duration.
- Keep app footage legible; no heavy overlays over core UI.
- Use one ambient motion per scene: map drift, image scale, stamp pulse, certificate settle, or contour-line travel.
- Final scene may fade down; earlier scenes rely on transitions for exits.

## What NOT To Do

- Do not make a pure slideshow of screenshots.
- Do not use purple-blue tech gradients, neon cyber styling, or generic SaaS cards.
- Do not cover the app UI with long marketing copy.
- Do not use identical pan/zoom motion on every scene.
- Do not imply live features that are not visible in the actual app footage.
